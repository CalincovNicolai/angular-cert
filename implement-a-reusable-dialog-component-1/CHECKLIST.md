- Open the app and create a quiz. Answer all questions and click on the "Submit" button.
- A dialog should show up with the following text:
  - The title must be "Are you sure?"
  - The body must be "Please confirm you want to submit your quiz"
  - The footer has two buttons, "OK" and "Not yet"
- Click on "Not yet". The dialog closes and nothing else happens.
- Click on "Submit" again. Then click on "OK". The application moves to the quiz result page and displays your score.
